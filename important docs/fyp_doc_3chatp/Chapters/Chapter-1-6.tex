\section{Intended Market of Product}

The intended audience of SmartExam is any university and university's associated organization where a supervision test involving a desktop system environment is commonly implemented, where more than 30 persons are often sitting together in one or more room(s), and the available current method cannot suffice the demand for a more formal/technical approach of supervised test management.

\subsection{Target Audience}
The main target market consists of Universities and Colleges that conduct CS (computer science), SWE (software engineering), IT (information technology), Cybersecurity and Data Science department(s). As of today, many universities still use manual attendance record and informal observation by the teacher to monitor their students during practical assessment in a lab computer setup. These types of tests generally involves code compiling, application setup or any operation on physical computer which is not directly supportable by a pure web-based system.

\subsection{Institutional Environment}
A Secondary target market could be technical training organizations that provide certification tests based on a physical computing lab setup, departmental assessment centres. For such organizations where a specific hardware environment is to be provisioned for each candidate, an operational process that allows tight management of the workstation allocated to each candidate, controlled environment and documented audit trail.

Institutions focusing purely on online-based assessments in students' individual environment using home-based computers are not intended for this platform. Similarly, programmes that offer tests based entirely on theoretical questions or Multiple choice questions do not fall within our targeted user group, as this product specializes on supervised on-site machine-based testing.
