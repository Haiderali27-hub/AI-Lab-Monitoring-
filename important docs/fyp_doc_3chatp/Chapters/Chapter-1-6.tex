\section{Intended Market of Product}

SmartExam targets institutions that conduct supervised practical exams in computer labs and need stronger operational accountability than manual methods can provide.

\subsection{Target Audience}
Universities and colleges offering computer science, software engineering, information technology, cybersecurity, and data science programs are the primary market. These departments run lab-based exams every semester. Students sit at physical workstations, compile code, and produce digital output under timed conditions. The existing approach --- paper attendance, informal invigilation, USB-based file collection --- does not scale cleanly beyond about thirty concurrent students. SmartExam replaces that approach with an integrated digital workflow.

\subsection{Institutional Environment}
A secondary market includes technical training institutes, certification centers, and departmental exam cells that manage batch assessments on physical machines. These environments need machine allocation, attendance control, restricted desktop usage, and post-session evidence review. SmartExam fits this profile precisely because it is built around structured lab operations rather than general course management.

Institutions that run MCQ-only online exams from home, or programs that do not use campus labs, are not the intended market. The platform is specifically designed for invigilated, on-campus, machine-bound practical examinations.
