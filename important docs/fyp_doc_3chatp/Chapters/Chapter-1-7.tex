\section{Intended Users of the Product}

SmartExam serves four user groups. Each group interacts with a different subset of the platform and carries different responsibilities during the exam lifecycle.

\subsection{Administrator}
Administrators own the operational configuration of the system. They create and deactivate user accounts, assign roles, register lab rooms, map physical machines to room seats, and control institutional-level settings. After exam sessions conclude, administrators pull system-wide audit reports for quality review and accreditation documentation. They do not create or grade exams --- that belongs to teachers.

\subsection{Teacher}
Teachers build and own their exams. They define titles, time limits, coding questions, theory questions, test cases, and allowed application lists. They assign students to their section, link the exam to a lab room, and release instructions before the session begins. After the exam, teachers review auto-graded results, inspect the plagiarism matrix, and sign off on final grades. They also serve as primary contacts if students raise disputes about their submissions.

\subsection{Invigilator}
Invigilators operate the live monitoring dashboard during an active session. Their screen shows a grid of student cards --- each with a real-time focus status indicator, a recent screenshot, and a process list. When a student's focus drops for more than a configurable threshold, the card turns red and an alert fires. The invigilator investigates and logs the incident directly in the system. The invigilator role may overlap with the teacher role or may be assigned separately by the administrator.

\subsection{Student}
Students access only their own scheduled exam session \cite{biometric1}. Before the exam begins, they complete face recognition verification on the WPF client. The client checks their registered HWID. If both checks pass, the exam environment launches in fullscreen lockdown. Students see their questions, write code or theory answers, and submit before the timer expires. They cannot access any other applications, switch windows, or alter their submission after it is locked.
