\chapter{SOFTWARE PROJECT PLAN}
\label{chap:spp}

This chapter details how we plan and manage the execution and delivery of the AI-Driven Lab Exam Monitoring and Management System across a two-semester final year project timeline \cite{spp1}. Developing twelve interconnected modules is not merely a programming task; it requires explicit coordination to ensure authentication, monitoring, submission, and reporting modules are implemented sequentially. Modules are closely linked: exam scheduling feeds the monitoring system, the live dashboard tracks incident events, and these incidents are compiled into a final report. Consequently, a delay in one module has a cascading effect on subsequent deliverables. This project plan provides a structured breakdown of deliverables, the scheduling framework, the critical path, and risk management strategies. Specifically, the chapter covers five categories of deliverables, scheduling arrangements based on Gantt chart, WBS, and CPM methods, and our risk management plan \cite{agile1}.
