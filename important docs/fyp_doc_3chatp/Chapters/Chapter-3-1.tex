\chapter{SOFTWARE PROJECT PLAN}
\label{chap:spp}

This chapter explains how we plan, schedule and control the project over two semesters \cite{spp1}. The twelve modules depend on each other: exam creation feeds eligibility, the monitoring client feeds the live dashboard, and both feed the final reports. A delay in an early module therefore delays the later ones. The chapter covers the deliverables, the project management plan (work breakdown, Gantt chart and critical path), the managerial process, and risk management.
