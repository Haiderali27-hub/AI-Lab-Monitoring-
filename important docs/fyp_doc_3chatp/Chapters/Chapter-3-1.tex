\chapter{SOFTWARE PROJECT PLAN}
\label{chap:spp}

This chapter discusses the planning and management of the execution and delivery of the AI-Driven Lab Exam Monitoring and Management System based on a two-semester final year project timeline \cite{spp1}. Developing twelve interconnected modules is not only a programming exercise but also requires explicit coordination so that the authentication, monitoring, submission and reporting modules are implemented sequentially. Modules are tightly coupled: the exam scheduling feeds into the monitoring system, the live dashboard tracks incident events, and these incidents are collated into a final report. Consequently, a delay in one module has a cascading effect on subsequent deliverables. This project plan provides a structured breakdown of deliverables, the scheduling framework, the critical path, and risk management strategies. Specifically, the chapter covers five categories of deliverables, scheduling arrangements based on Gantt chart, WBS, and CPM methods, and our risk management plan \cite{agile1}.
