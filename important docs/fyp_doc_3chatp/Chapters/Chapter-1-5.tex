\section{Benefits of Proposed System}

The proposed system offers a comprehensive set of integrated administrative and operational capabilities designed to address the challenges of practical laboratory assessments. By incorporating role-based user management, the platform supports distinct workflows for administrators, instructors, invigilators, and students, allowing the institution to align digital permissions with academic responsibilities and minimize unauthorized access to exam configurations. Authorized academic staff can configure exams, assign dates, durations, and instructions, and map sessions to specific student cohorts and physical laboratories, which enhances planning consistency and mitigates pre-exam coordination errors \cite{eassess1}. Furthermore, by linking digital exam sessions directly to approved physical workstations and laboratory locations, the system establishes a secure connection between the software environment and the physical testing space, enforcing controlled, time-bound exam execution that ensures all candidates operate under identical, standardized conditions.

In addition to planning utilities, the system provides real-time supervision and security mechanisms through a centralized live monitoring dashboard that gives invigilators instant visibility into student activity, session statuses, and workstation connectivity. This centralized view mitigates the difficulty of managing large student groups in a computer lab, allowing invigilators to quickly identify inactive sessions, workstation disconnects, or technical discrepancies. To support academic integrity, the platform features automated alerting and structured event logging that captures suspicious client-side events, such as unauthorized window shifts or local system anomalies, and preserves them as auditable evidence. This digital evidence trail is paired with an organized submission management workflow that automatically links student code outputs with the correct candidate and exam session, eliminating the risk of lost files or mismatched submissions that frequently occur with manual collection methods.

Following the conclusion of an exam session, the platform compiles consolidated reports and logs to facilitate post-exam review, grade verification, and administrative audit trails. Instructors and department administrators can review attendance records, logged incident timelines, session histories, and file submission metadata, providing a clear reference for grading disputes and helping institutions refine their testing procedures over time \cite{spp1}. Ultimately, the proposed solution goes beyond isolated utilities like secure browsers or simple submission portals by providing an end-to-end environment tailored specifically for practical laboratory examinations. By unifying scheduling, workstation control, live supervision, and post-exam reporting into a single coherent framework, the system delivers an academically rigorous, operationally realistic, and institutionally defensible platform for modern technical education \cite{sommerville}.
